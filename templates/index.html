🌷 A LITTLE KNOWLEDGE, A LOT OF CARE

Getting to know PCOS

Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal condition that can affect how the ovaries work. It can look different from person to person, and having one symptom does not automatically mean someone has PCOS.

🌸 What might you notice?

Some people experience irregular or missed periods, acne, increased coarse facial or body hair, scalp hair thinning, or changes in weight. Symptoms vary widely.

🩺 How is PCOS diagnosed?

A healthcare professional considers your history, symptoms and appropriate tests, while checking for other possible causes. A website quiz or machine-learning result cannot diagnose PCOS.

🌿 What can help?

Depending on your needs, care may include sustainable food and movement habits, support for symptoms, and medication prescribed by a clinician. There is no single approach that suits everyone.

💗 When to seek support

If your periods are persistently irregular or absent, or you are worried about symptoms, consider talking with a qualified healthcare professional. You deserve care without shame or blame.

Gentle reminder: PCOS is not caused by a personal failure, and lifestyle advice is not a substitute for individual medical care.

Small ways to care for yourself

Wellness is not about doing everything perfectly. Choose one small thing that feels kind to you today.

☕ Make a comfort ritual

Sip water or a warm drink, get comfortable, and give yourself a few quiet minutes.

🌿 Let your body rest

Gentle stretching, a slow walk, or simply resting can be enough. Follow what feels right for you.

📖 Write one kind thought

Try: “I do not have to be productive every moment to deserve rest.”

🩷 Track how you feel

Notice your mood and energy without judging yourself. Patterns can take time to understand.
